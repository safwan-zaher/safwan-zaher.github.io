About

I work on the security of systems that get deployed faster than they get understood. Three questions occupy me right now: whether agentic AI tools behave the way they declare they will, whether deployable LLM guardrails survive multi-turn attacks in a low-resource language, and whether cheap backdoor defenses defend anything once you hold clean accuracy fixed.

Before security I built distributed query-processing systems. My undergraduate thesis on top-k dominating queries over uncertain databases was published at IEEE ICCIT 2025. I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

Research

Graduate research (remote) · Mentor: Dr. Ozgur Ozmen, SCAI, Arizona State University

Pre-deployment behavioral vetting of agentic artifacts — agent skills and MCP servers — checked against the capabilities they declare. The gap between declaration and behaviour is where supply-chain risk enters an agent stack.

Project complete; paper in preparation for submission.

Dept. of CSE, Southeast University · with undergraduate researchers I supervise

  • Built a native Bangla/Banglish multi-turn dataset with turn-level harm labels, hard benign controls, and dual annotation; removed a refusal-text leak that had let a keyword baseline match a fine-tuned guard.
  • Benchmarked four deployable guardrails — Llama Guard 3, ShieldGemma, WildGuard, and an LLM-judge baseline. All score 95–100% in English and degrade sharply in Bangla, with a further drop when the guard reads the full conversation rather than only the final turn.
  • Dataset expansion and manuscript in progress; targeting ACL/EMNLP or NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks.
  • LLM Safety
  • Guardrails
  • Low-resource NLP
  • Red-teaming

Dept. of CSE, Southeast University · with undergraduate researchers I supervise

  • Benchmarked ten preprocessing filters across 55 configurations on a ResNet-18 CIFAR-10 classifier backdoored by BadNets, Blended, WaNet, and FIBA.
  • Attack-success reduction turns out not to be evidence that a filter works: eight of ten filters cut stealthy-attack ASR by over 20 points, but each cost more than 5 points of clean accuracy.
  • Proposed a clean-accuracy viability floor as a reporting standard for this class of defense. Under review at IEEE ICEEICT 2027.
